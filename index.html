How it works

Three compact steps to turn raw Git diffs into review-ready pull requests.

1

Bring your diff

Paste your staged changes, branch diff, or link a GitHub PR. SpeakPR parses syntax, file paths, and AST modifications automatically.

2

Explain your reasoning

Speak for 30 seconds into Wispr Flow or type normally. Explain your architecture decisions, edge cases, and alternatives considered.

3

Review a clearer PR

Receive structured PR summaries, customer-ready changelogs, and evidence-backed flags highlighting any discrepancies between diff and intent.

Why voice?

After three hours of debugging, nobody types four paragraphs of context. Speaking takes 25 seconds.
